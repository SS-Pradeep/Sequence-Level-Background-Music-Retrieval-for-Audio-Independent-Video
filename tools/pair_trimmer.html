Space play/pause · ←→ one frame · Shift+←→ one second · I/O set start/end of the selected part · A add a part at the playhead · Del remove the selected part · P preview · S save · X exclude · N/B next/previous pair. Drag the edges of the blue regions on the waveform to adjust a part; click inside one to select it. Example for a 1 min clip: set the end of part 1 to 30 s, then add parts at 35 s and 53 s and set their ends to 45 s and 59 s (or type 0-30, 35-45, 53-59). Each part is saved as its own pair (_p1, _p2, …), never glued together. Cuts snap to video frames; the music is cut at the same instants, so they stay in sync.
