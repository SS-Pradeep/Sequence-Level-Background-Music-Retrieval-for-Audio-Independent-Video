# LLM tags + tag-overlap retrieval (D141)

Generated by `python -m bgm.tagging.run_all eval` (src/bgm/tagging/). Music windows (10 s, 5 s hop) scored by Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, scenes (4-8 frames of the silent video only) by Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct, both 4-bit nf4, over the fixed 42-tag vocabulary (src/bgm/tagging/tags.json). One question per group and paraphrase (A; B reworded with the options reversed); confidence = the model's probability of answering with each option, calibrated by subtracting each option's mean log-prob over all items of the side (no labels); energy / tempo keep the top option, the other groups up to 3 with confidence ≥ τ; a tag is kept only when A and B both select it (confidence = mean). final = ensemble cosine + λ · weighted-Jaccard(scene tags, window tags). V2M segment retrieval on the saved 10-head ensemble scores of results/bidir_retrieval.md (title split, 5 folds × 3 disjoint ensembles = 15 runs). λ ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0} and group weights tuned per fold on that fold's val split only (val MRR, first grid point wins ties, λ = 0 first). Mean ± std over the 15 runs; [CI] and Δ: 95% film-group cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples) on per-query values averaged over a fold's 3 ensembles; Δ paired against λ = 0; * = CI excludes 0. Ties share ranks.

## Original dataset

Baseline reproduces the saved V2M ranks of results/bidir_retrieval.md for 100.0% of queries (0.11% of base queries have a tie). 2,552 test segments, 218 film groups.
