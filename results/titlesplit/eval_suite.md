## 3. Transitions (test concatenations)

50 test concatenations per fold (3–4 clips of different films joined end to end, `concat_sets_title.json`); the true music switches are the joins. F1: one-to-one matching of predicted file switches to joins within ±1.5 / ±2.0 s. Deviation (MIREX style, s): T→P = median distance from each join to the nearest predicted switch, P→T = from each predicted switch to the nearest join. TBIoU: each true piece's best temporal IoU with a predicted piece, averaged. Smoothness: switches / min (truth: 2.5), jumps / min (same file, non-contiguous), median loudness jump at the rendered soundtrack's seams (LU, ±3 s; the original's seams are the joins, rendered the same way). DTW: cosine-distance DTW between the per-second CLAP embeddings of the produced and the original soundtrack, per path step (0 = the original music).

Entropy: of the distribution of chosen files, divided by log(gallery size) (1 = every file equally often). Coverage: share of the fold's gallery files chosen at least once.
