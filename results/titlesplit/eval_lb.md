# LanguageBind score of the final system (D117, title split)

Generated by `python -m bgm.analysis.eval_lb report` (src/bgm/analysis/eval_lb.py; encoders in src/bgm/features/languagebind.py) from runs/titlesplit/eval_lb/. The soundtracks are the ones the evaluation suite (D111, results/titlesplit/eval_suite.md) decoded and stored per segment; nothing is retrained or tuned. LanguageBind_Video_FT and LanguageBind_Audio_FT (frozen, fp16; config 97c5c032736e). 982 natural test videos; tables: mean ± std over 15 runs (5 folds × 3 disjoint 10-head ensembles); [95% CI]: per-video values averaged over seeds, cluster bootstrap over film groups, 10,000 resamples.

* **LB**: cosine between the LanguageBind embedding of the whole video (8 frames spread over the clip) and of the whole rendered soundtrack (what `bgm render` plays, before beat sync).
* **tw-LB** (time-weighted): each segment's cosine (its 8 frames vs its chosen music window, widened to ≥ 5 s as for IB), averaged with the segment lengths as weights.
* seg-LB: the same average without the weights. IB: the suite's ImageBind score (duration-weighted), for comparison.

Higher = the music suits the picture more in LanguageBind's joint space.
